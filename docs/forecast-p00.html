How to read this

The history shows no movement at all, so there is no trend to fit: the program uses its refresh prior — Exophase refreshes its counts every few hours, each refresh brings new tracked players who hold fewer trophies, so rates are shaded down for this short horizon (Chapter 1: 1.25% → 0.82% median). The trophy every tracked player holds ("Just Like Paradise", 100%) stays at 99.99%.

Method in one line: forecast earners and players-tracked separately and divide; collapse the history to distinct reads; drop seeded/stale reads; fit log counts on log-time (last 24 h); constrain the story funnel (Ch1 ≥ Ch2 ≥ Ch3 ≥ Ch4 ≥ final ≥ 100% ≥ Platinum); spreads are prediction intervals on the logit scale.
